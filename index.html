Lifestyle & Experiences

Golf Tours:

South Africa’s coasts, from Cape Town to the Garden Route, are golf havens. Avodah turns a golf trip into a tailored journey across dramatic Atlantic links and lush parkland courses—minus the logistics.

Curated Course Access: We provide coveted access and pre-booked tee times at championship venues and exclusive private clubs along the coast and through the Winelands.

  • Seamless Logistics: Travel in absolute comfort with dedicated, luxury transport to and from every course, ensuring your focus remains entirely on your game.
  • Tailored Itineraries: Whether you desire a pure links challenge, a mix of parkland aesthetics, or a combination tour that blends golf with wine tasting, your itinerary is designed specifically for you.
  • Personalized Support: Rely on our experienced team for assistance with local caddies, equipment handling, and securing any necessary club amenities.

Taste of the Cape

Culinary Journeys:

Indulge in the true “Taste of the Cape.” Avodah curates personalized culinary excursions, leading you deep into South Africa’s vibrant food culture. Explore the dynamic fusion of flavors—from traditional Cape Malay spice routes to world-class fine dining—and experience the region’s artistry firsthand.

Bespoke Wine & Food Pairings: Access exclusive, private tastings with acclaimed vintners in the Cape Winelands, perfectly pairing the region’s best wines with locally sourced ingredients.

  • The Artisanal Connection: Go beyond the restaurant and meet the acclaimed chefs, bakers, and local producers shaping South Africa’s diverse culinary landscape.
  • Fusion Flavor Immersion: Journey through a smorgasbord of cultural influences, from authentic, historical neighbourhood cuisine to cutting-edge fusion dishes by international experts.
  • Private Dining Access: Enjoy reservations at Cape Town’s most sought-after establishments, ensuring a seamless and celebrated epicurean experience.

The Heart of the Wild

Safari & Nature:

Embark on the adventure of a lifetime, reimagined for the discerning traveler. Avodah provides bespoke access to South Africa’s most exclusive private game reserves and untouched wilderness areas. Witness Africa’s iconic wildlife, including the majestic Big Five—lion, leopard, rhino, elephant, and buffalo—from the absolute comfort and luxury of world-class lodges.

Exclusive Private Reserves: We secure bookings in premier reserves known for ethical wildlife viewing, low visitor volume, and exceptional photographic opportunities.

  • World-Class Field Guides: Your journey is led by highly knowledgeable, experienced field guides and trackers who ensure deep immersion and understanding of the ecosystem.
  • Bespoke Viewing: Enjoy tailor-made game drives and nature walks designed around your specific interests, whether they are birding, photography, or focusing on specific species.
  • Seamless Transition: Experience the peace of mind of Avodah’s luxury transport, providing comfortable, reliable transfers even to the most remote bush settings.

The Ultimate Craft Beer
Tour Experience

Beer Tours:

Explore the thriving local beer scene on an unforgettable guided tour. Whether you’re a seasoned hop-head or new to the world of brewing, our Beer Tour offers a behind-the-scenes look at the craftsmanship, passion, and unique flavours defining local brewing culture.

What You’ll Experience:

  • Three Premier Stops: Visit a mix of established brewing giants and innovative microbreweries.
  • Behind-the-Scenes Access: Tour the mash tuns, fermenters, and bottling lines—learn about the ingredients and processes that transform simple grains into complex brews.
  • Guided Tasting: Sample a diverse flight of four unique beers at each location, ranging from crisp lagers and hoppy IPAs to rich stouts and sour ales. Learn how to properly evaluate aroma, body, and finish.
  • Local History & Culture: Discover the stories and personalities behind the brews, including the history of beer-making in the region.
